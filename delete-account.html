Delete your account

You can delete your Cadet AI account and all of its data at any time — in the app, or online without the app. Here’s how, what gets deleted, and how long it takes.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Cadet AI and go to Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data right away.

Option 2 — Online, without the app

You do not need the app installed to delete your account. Email support@cadetai.co.uk (the link opens a pre-filled message) from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”, and ask us to delete it. For a cadet under 18, a parent or guardian can email on their behalf. We may ask a question or two to confirm it’s really you, then we permanently delete the account and all its data. We complete deletion within one month of confirming your request, and usually within a few days.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account: your profile (name, organisation, unit or school, rank, star level), your progress (quiz results, XP, badges, awards, streaks), your TANGO chat history, your answer feedback, any leaderboard nickname, and your settings. Your “age verified” status is removed with the account.

We may keep minimal records only where the law requires (for example, proof of a purchase, which UK tax law requires us to keep for up to six years), with no profile data attached. Everything else is erased.

Your Cadet AI Premium subscription

If you subscribed to Cadet AI Premium, deleting your account does not cancel or refund your Google Play subscription — the subscription is managed by Google Play, separately from your Cadet AI account. To stop being charged you must cancel it yourself in Google Play: open the Play Store app → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Cadet AI → Cancel subscription (or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the period you’ve already paid for. We recommend cancelling the subscription before you delete your account.

Deleting just some of your data

You don’t have to delete your whole account to clear specific data — for example, you can clear your TANGO chat history from within the app. To request deletion of specific data, email support@cadetai.co.uk.

For full detail on what we collect and your rights, see our Privacy Policy.
