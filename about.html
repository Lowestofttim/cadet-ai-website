About Cadet AI

Cadet AI is an independent revision app and guide library for UK Army Cadet Force and Combined Cadet Force learners aged 13 and over.

Who we are

Cadet AI is operated by MONKEYZOO LTD, a UK company. We build tools that help cadets understand training subjects, practise recall and arrive better prepared for instruction and assessment.

Cadet AI is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Ministry of Defence, the British Army, the ACF, the CCF or any official cadet organisation. It supplements training; it does not replace qualified instruction, local orders or official publications.

Editorial policy

Our public guides are written for a clear question a cadet, parent or volunteer might ask. The Cadet AI team checks factual statements against first-party government or official organisation sources where those sources are publicly available. Each article displays its review date and links to the principal sources used.

  • Accuracy: we prefer primary sources and clearly separate official facts from descriptions of Cadet AI.
  • Safety: safety-critical, weapons-related and practical training instructions must be confirmed with a qualified instructor. Our guides do not override local direction.
  • Independence: we do not imply endorsement by an official cadet organisation.
  • AI assistance: automated tools may help with drafting, checking or formatting, but pages are manually reviewed before publication.
  • Updates: material changes receive a new review date. Older pages may be corrected sooner when an error is reported.

Corrections and feedback

Found something unclear or out of date? Email support@cadetai.co.uk with the page address and the change you recommend. We will check it against the best available primary source.
