Why Cadet AI

Standards Win. So Train to the Standard.

Anyone can show up. Few show up sharp. Cadet AI is built for the cadet who wants to be the one staff trust, the one their section looks to, the one who passes assessments first time. It's a study-aid app for UK Army Cadet Force (ACF) and Combined Cadet Force (CCF) cadets aged 13 and over — your pocket revision drill for everything from Drill and Turnout to Fieldcraft, First Aid and Navigation. No fluff. Just the knowledge that earns rank.

Meet TANGO

Your Training Assistant

TANGO is the AI mentor built into Cadet AI — and your friendly mascot. Ask any training question and TANGO answers from official MOD Army Cadet training pamphlets, then cites the source so you can check the reference yourself. Trustworthy answers grounded in the real material, not made up. If TANGO can't find a match in the pamphlets, it tells you — rather than inventing one.

Prefer to listen on the move? Switch on read-aloud and let TANGO brief you out loud. It's like having a patient instructor in your pocket who always shows their working.

Progression

Earn Every Rank. Track Every Star.

Progress here is earned, never handed over. Every quiz you answer correctly banks XP. XP builds your level, your level carries military ranks, and you unlock star-level awards from Basic all the way through to 4-Star. Behind the scenes, your progress is tracked against the Army Proficiency Certificate (APC) syllabus — so the game you're playing is the training you actually need to know, and you always know exactly where you stand and what to drill next.

Rewards

Collect the Honours. Build Your Reputation.

Cadet AI rewards graft. Stack up 100-plus badges, chase 35 real-world-style awards — think Duke of Edinburgh, marksmanship and first aid — and hold your daily streak like it's a kit inspection. Unlock collectible cosmetics to kit out your TANGO and show how far you've come. When you want to test yourself against others, opt in to national and unit leaderboards. Leaderboards are off by default — you decide if and when you join the board.

For cadets — reassuring for parents & staff

Discipline Includes Discipline About Data.

Cadet AI carries no ads, no third-party analytics trackers, and we never sell your data. Under-13s cannot create an account. Leaderboards are opt-in and off by default, so a cadet is never listed unless they choose to be. Parents and guardians get a focused, safe study tool; section and detachment staff get a study aid that points cadets straight back to the official pamphlets. Read the full privacy policy any time.

An independent study aid — not an official product

Cadet AI is an independent study aid. It is not an official MOD, Army Cadet Force, Combined Cadet Force or Cadet Forces product, and it is not endorsed by them. It simply helps cadets revise using publicly available official training pamphlets — with sources cited so you can always check the facts yourself.
