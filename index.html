Why Cadet AI

Standards Win. So Train to the Standard.

Anyone can show up. Few show up sharp. Cadet AI is built for the cadet who wants to be the one staff trust, the one their section looks to, the one who passes assessments first time. It's a study-aid app for UK Army Cadet Force (ACF) and Combined Cadet Force (CCF) cadets aged 13 and over: your pocket revision drill for everything from Drill and Turnout to Fieldcraft, First Aid and Navigation. No fluff. Just the knowledge that earns rank.

Meet TANGO

Your Training Assistant

TANGO is the AI mentor built into Cadet AI, and your friendly mascot. Ask any training question and TANGO answers in plain English, grounded in your cadet training and never made up. If it's not sure, it tells you rather than inventing an answer, so you can trust what you're revising.

Pick your TANGO from the 20-character roster (5 characters on the Free plan, 15 with Plus and all 20 with Pro), then take it everywhere you train. Each character has a curated level 1-20 upgrade path, earned through genuine TANGO chat, quiz and subject progress.

TANGO progression

20 TANGOs. 400 Mobile-Ready Level States.

Choose your favourite unlocked TANGO and keep it with you across the app. As you use TANGO chat, answer questions, cover more subjects and build real learning momentum, your selected character can rank up through a clear level 1-20 visual ladder.

Locked levels stay visible with honest next-step requirements, so cadets always know what they are working towards. The aim is simple: make revision feel collectible, earned and worth coming back to.

Progression

Earn Every Rank. Track Every Star.

Progress here is earned, never handed over. Every quiz you answer correctly banks XP. XP builds your level, your level carries military ranks, and you unlock star-level awards from Basic all the way through to 4-Star. Behind the scenes, your progress is tracked across the cadet training syllabus, so the game you're playing is the training you actually need to know. You always know exactly where you stand and what to drill next.

Sample Drill & Turnout

Why do cadets practise drill?

Drill teaches teamwork, discipline, bearing and confidence. Cadet AI turns that into short revision reps, clear explanations and follow-up questions.

Sample question When standing at attention, why should you brace rather than lock your knees?

To stay steady and reduce the risk of feeling faint. TANGO keeps answers practical and reminds cadets to follow their instructors.

Premium subscription

Manage or Cancel Anytime.

Your Cadet AI Plus or Pro subscription is billed securely through your Google Play account, so you're always in full control. Cancel in a couple of taps whenever you like, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play, so you cancel from your Google account on any device:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app, or go to play.google.com.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Cadet AI, tap Cancel subscription, and follow the prompts.
Open Google Play subscriptions

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep your subscription until your current billing period ends.

For cadets, reassuring for parents & staff

Discipline Includes Discipline About Data.

Cadet AI carries no ads, no third-party analytics trackers, and we never sell your data. Under-13s cannot create an account. Leaderboards are opt-in and off by default, so a cadet is never listed unless they choose to be. Parents and guardians get a focused, safe study tool; section and detachment staff get a study aid that encourages cadets to confirm the detail with their instructors. Read the full privacy policy any time.

An independent study aid, not an official product

Cadet AI is an independent study aid. It is not an official MOD, Army Cadet Force, Combined Cadet Force or Cadet Forces product, and it is not endorsed by them. It simply helps cadets revise the cadet training syllabus, and always encourages you to confirm the detail with your instructors.
