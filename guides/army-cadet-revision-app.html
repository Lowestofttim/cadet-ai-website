Army Cadet Revision App

A good ACF or CCF revision app should help you practise recall, cover the subjects you actually study and make its limits clear.

Reviewed 24 October 2026. Product details checked by the Cadet AI team; official progression context checked against Army Cadets. How we review our guides.

What should an Army Cadet revision app do?

Look for short practice sessions, questions across the syllabus, explanations after mistakes and a clear route back to your own notes or instructor. An app is most useful between parade nights: it should reinforce training rather than pretend to replace it.

What it does not do

Cadet AI is independent and is not an official MOD, ACF or CCF product. It cannot sign off training, replace practical instruction or decide local procedures. For weapons, safety-critical or unit-specific questions, follow your qualified instructor and official local direction.

A simple revision routine

  1. Pick one subject you covered recently.
  2. Answer a short quiz without looking at notes.
  3. Review every wrong answer and explain the right answer in your own words.
  4. Ask TANGO for a simpler explanation if a concept still does not make sense.
  5. Repeat the weak questions a few days later.
