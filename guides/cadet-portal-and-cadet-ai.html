Cadet Portal vs Cadet AI

They are separate tools with separate jobs: Cadet Portal is an official service; Cadet AI is an independent revision app.

Reviewed 24 October 2026. Cadet Portal details checked against the official Army Cadets website. How we review our guides.

The short answer

Cadet Portal is the official Army Cadets portal used for information connected to a cadet's participation, including their record and available activities. Cadet AI is a separate study aid for revising ACF and CCF knowledge through quizzes and the TANGO assistant.

Use Cadet Portal for official information

  • Your official cadet information and progress.
  • Activities or opportunities made available through Army Cadets.
  • Details that your detachment or contingent expects you to use there.

If an official record looks wrong or you cannot access the portal, speak to your unit. Cadet AI cannot see or alter Cadet Portal data.

Use Cadet AI for revision practice

  • Short quizzes across 13 training subjects.
  • Explanations from TANGO when a topic is unclear.
  • Practice from Basic through 4-Star subject levels.
  • Motivation through XP, badges, ranks and optional leaderboards.

Do the accounts connect?

No. Cadet AI does not ask for your Cadet Portal password, does not sign in to Cadet Portal and cannot import official records. Never enter an official portal password into an unrelated app or website.

Which one should I use?

Use the official portal for official records and activities. Use Cadet AI when you want extra practice between training sessions. For assessment standards, safety or practical instruction, your qualified staff and current official material always take priority.
