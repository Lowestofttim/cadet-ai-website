Cadet Kit List

What’s issued, what you actually need to buy, and the small things everybody forgets on their first weekend away.

First, the good news: uniform is issued

You do not buy uniform. It is issued by the unit once a cadet is enrolled, and exchanged as they grow. Nobody needs to turn up to a first parade night in anything special — comfortable clothes and trainers are fine. Be wary of anyone suggesting you need to buy kit before you have even spoken to a unit.

Parade night

  • Uniform once issued, clean and pressed, with boots.
  • A notebook and a pen that works.
  • A drink.
  • Any medication, and make sure the unit knows about it.

A weekend away

Units issue a specific kit list for each weekend and that list is the one to follow — it will reflect the activity and the weather. Typically it includes:

  • Washkit and a towel.
  • Spare socks, more than seems necessary. Then more again.
  • Warm layers, and waterproofs.
  • A sleeping bag, and a roll mat if told to bring one.
  • A torch — a head torch is far better than a handheld one — with spare batteries.
  • Black bin bags. They waterproof a bergen from the inside and cost nothing.

Annual camp

Camp is the same idea for longer, so the list grows rather than changes: more socks, more layers, more washkit. Add a padlock if lockers are used, a cheap watch, and enough spending money for the NAAFI or the trip out if there is one. Label everything — identical issued kit in a room of thirty cadets goes missing constantly.

The things people always forget

  • Spare socks and a spare pair of boots laces. Wet feet ruin a weekend faster than anything else.
  • A head torch with working batteries. Not a phone torch — the phone will be flat.
  • Blister plasters. Deal with a hot spot early and it never becomes a blister.
  • A pen. Sounds trivial until there is a lesson and nobody has one.

What not to bring

Anything valuable, anything you would be upset to lose, and anything on the unit’s prohibited list — which will be made clear in advance. If in doubt, ask the unit rather than guessing. Knives and similar items are controlled: never bring anything of that sort unless the unit has explicitly instructed it as part of the kit list for an activity.

Quick answers

Cadet kit: FAQs

Do you have to buy cadet uniform?
No. Uniform is issued by the unit once a cadet is enrolled, and exchanged as they grow. You should not buy uniform before speaking to a unit.
What should you wear to a first parade night?
Comfortable clothes you can move in and trainers or boots. Uniform is not needed until it has been issued.
What is the most commonly forgotten item?
Spare socks, and a head torch with working batteries. Wet feet and no light spoil a weekend faster than anything else. Blister plasters and a working pen are close behind.
Who decides the kit list for a weekend or camp?
The unit does, and their list is the one to follow because it reflects the specific activity and the weather. If anything is unclear, ask the unit rather than guessing.
