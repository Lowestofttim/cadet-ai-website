What's inside

Everything You Need to Earn It.

A study-aid built around one idea: learn the real material, prove it, and watch it turn into rank. Here's the full kit.

🎯

TANGO AI — Answers You Can Trust

An in-app AI training assistant that answers straight from official MOD Army Cadet pamphlets and cites its sources every time — so you learn the real material, not a guess. Optional voice read-aloud for hands-free briefing.

📑

Source-Grounded, Not Made Up

Every TANGO answer is tied to the official pamphlet it came from. You see the reference and can check it yourself — and if there's no match in the pamphlets, TANGO says so rather than inventing an answer.

🧠

Quizzes — Basic to 4-Star

Test yourself across all ten real cadet subjects — Drill & Turnout, Fieldcraft, First Aid, Navigation, Shooting, Skill at Arms, Military Knowledge, Comms & IT, Keeping Active and Community Engagement — at every star level, growing with you.

🔁

Daily Challenge & Retry

A fresh daily challenge keeps your edge sharp, and instant retry turns every wrong answer into a rep that sticks. Consistency is what separates the sharp from the rest.

⭐

XP, Levels & Military Ranks

Bank XP for correct answers, level up, and rise through military ranks. Progress is earned and visible, so you always know how hard you've worked — and how far there is to go.

📈

Mapped to the APC Syllabus

Your progress tracks against the Army Proficiency Certificate syllabus and unlocks star-level awards from Basic to 4-Star, so your revision lines up exactly with what cadets are actually assessed on.

🎖️

100+ Badges & 35 Awards

Earn over 100 badges and chase 35 real-world-style awards — including Duke of Edinburgh, marksmanship and first aid themes — plus daily streaks and collectible cosmetics to kit out your TANGO.

🏆

Opt-In Leaderboards

Pit yourself against the best on national and unit leaderboards when you're ready. Leaderboards are opt-in and off by default — you decide if and when you want to compete.

📋

Quick Reference & Drill Practice

Fast Quick Reference for when you need a fact at a glance, plus drill practice to keep your turnout and procedure parade-ground tight.
