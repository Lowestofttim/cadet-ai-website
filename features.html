What's inside

Everything You Need to Earn It.

A study-aid built around one idea: learn the real material, prove it, and watch it turn into rank. Here's the full kit.

🎯

TANGO AI: Answers You Can Trust

An in-app AI study assistant that sticks to your cadet training and won't make things up — if it's not sure, it says so, so you learn it right, not a guess.

📑

No Guessing, No Made-Up Answers

TANGO sticks to what it knows from your cadet training. If it isn't sure, it tells you rather than inventing an answer, so you can trust what you revise.

🧠

Quizzes, Basic to 4-Star

Test yourself across all ten real cadet subjects (Drill & Turnout, Fieldcraft, First Aid, Navigation, Shooting, Skill at Arms, Military Knowledge, Comms & IT, Keeping Active and Community Engagement) at every star level.

🔁

Daily TANGO Challenge & Retry

A fresh daily challenge of ten questions across all subjects keeps your edge sharp, and instant retry turns every wrong answer into a rep that sticks, with each answer explained.

⭐

XP, Levels & Military Ranks

Bank XP for correct answers, level up, and rise through military ranks. Progress is earned and visible, so you always know how hard you've worked, and how far there is to go.

📈

Built Around Your Star Levels

Your progress tracks across the cadet training syllabus and unlocks star-level awards from Basic to 4-Star, so your revision lines up with what cadets actually learn at each level.

🔊

Read-Aloud & Neural Voice

Switch on read-aloud and TANGO briefs you out loud, hands-free. On Plus or Pro, TANGO speaks in its character's own lifelike neural voice, plus celebration sounds when you rank up.

🤖

20 Collectible TANGO Characters

Pick which of the 20 TANGO characters represents you and take it everywhere you train, with a cosmetic store on the way to kit yours out.

🎖️

100+ Badges & 35 Awards

Earn over 100 badges and chase 35 in-app awards inspired by real cadet honours (marksmanship, first aid, Duke of Edinburgh themes and more), plus daily streaks to keep your habit alive.

🏆

Opt-In Leaderboards

Pit yourself against the best on national and unit leaderboards, by week, month or all-time, when you're ready. Leaderboards are opt-in and off by default; you decide if and when to compete.

📋

Quick Reference & Drill Practice

Fast Quick Reference for when you need a fact at a glance, plus drill practice to keep your turnout and procedure parade-ground tight.

📝

Timed Mock Exams

Sit a timed, multi-topic mock drawn from the question bank, then get marked instantly with a per-subject breakdown of where you're strong and what to revise. Plus includes one a day; Pro is unlimited.

📊

Coaching & Readiness Insights

Pro turns your own quiz history into a personal coaching plan: a readiness score, per-subject mastery and the areas to focus on next — plus smart mistake review that resurfaces the questions you got wrong, spaced over time so they stick.

📥

Train Offline

Download quizzes for offline use so you can keep revising with no signal — perfect for camps, the field and the back of the minibus.

💎

Free to Use, Plus & Pro to Go Further

Cadet AI is free to download and use. Two optional plans go further: Plus (£3.99/mo) lifts your daily TANGO and quiz limits and adds neural voice and mock assessments; Pro (£9.99/mo) adds 200 messages a day, unlimited quizzes and mock exams, smart mistake review and exclusive TANGO voices. Billed via Google Play, cancel anytime.
